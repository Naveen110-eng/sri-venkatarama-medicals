Popular Medicine Brands & Uses

General product information for patient awareness

CROCIN®
Paracetamol tablets (check exact strength on pack)

General use

Paracetamol products are used for temporary relief of fever and mild-to-moderate pain. Different Crocin variants may contain additional ingredients, so check the exact pack.

Important care

Do not combine multiple paracetamol-containing products. Follow the pack and clinician's instructions; ask a pharmacist if unsure.

Source: Crocin product information

ALLEGRA®
Fexofenadine hydrochloride tablets (various strengths)

General use

An antihistamine indicated for relief of symptoms associated with allergic rhinitis and chronic idiopathic urticaria, as described in its product information.

Important care

Use only as directed by a registered medical practitioner or pharmacist. Check other medicines and health conditions before use.

Source: Sanofi India product information

BECOSULES®
B-Complex Forte with Vitamin C capsules

General use

A vitamin supplement indicated for patients with deficiencies of, or increased requirements for, Vitamin B-complex and Vitamin C.

Important care

Supplements are not a substitute for a balanced diet or medical assessment. Ask a healthcare professional whether it is suitable.

Source: Pfizer product label

Medical information notice: These are examples, not a treatment recommendation or a list of medicines suitable for every patient. Brand variants and strengths differ.
